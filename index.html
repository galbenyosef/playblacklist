Modes

Create a room, pick the rules, and play - with friends, strangers or bots.

Teams

Team deathmatch

Two squads, one scoreboard. Spawns are picked away from the enemy, so fights happen on the map - not on your back.

Solo

Deathmatch

Everyone against everyone. Headshots and positioning decide it: torso, arm and leg hits all deal different damage.

Rounds

Bomb defusal

Terrorists plant at site A or B; Counter-Terrorists defuse it at the bomb before the 40-second fuse runs out. No respawns mid-round, sides swap at half. On Forge and Zook.

Always a match

Bot fill

Quiet server? Bots join empty slots and make way when real players arrive. Toggle it per room.

Arsenal

A pick from the arsenal. Build your loadout across five slots, then make it yours with weapon skins, characters and gloves.

And many more. Every class has more weapons and skins to unlock in the game, from Common standard issue to Legendary.

Before you play

  • Desktop browserChrome, Edge or Firefox with WebGL 2. Phones and tablets aren't supported yet.
  • Keyboard & mouseClick the game to lock the cursor; press Esc to get it back.
  • One-time downloadThe first load downloads the game; after that it starts from your browser cache.

About

Blacklist

Blacklist is a free tactical multiplayer FPS that runs in your browser: team deathmatch, deathmatch and bomb defusal on eight maps, a full arsenal with skins, and bots that fill empty rooms.

These credits are in the game as well - open the (i) button next to Settings in the lobby.
